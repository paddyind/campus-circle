# User Guide

Welcome to the CampusCircle User Guide! This document will help you get started with the platform and make the most of its features.

## Registration

To register for a CampusCircle account, click the "Register" button on the homepage. You will be asked to provide your name, email address, and a password.

## Login

Once you have registered, you can log in to your account using your email address and password.

## Dashboard

After logging in, you will be taken to your dashboard, where you can view upcoming events, manage your children's profiles, and update your account information.

## Events

You can view a list of all upcoming events on the "Events" page. To register for an event, click the "Register" button next to the event you wish to attend.
